BIM y medición

Check a model against the EIR and raise issues

Open the information requirements, check a delivered model against them for naming, classification, level of detail and required properties, raise a BCF issue on every gap and sign off only what passes.

Cómo funciona, paso a paso

4 pasos a lo largo de la plataforma - qué haces en cada uno y por qué importa.

1

Open the requirements

Matriz EIR (ISO 19650)

Open the information requirements the model is meant to satisfy - the naming convention, classification, level of information need and the properties each element must carry.

Por que: You cannot check a model against a standard you have not opened. Starting from the actual requirements turns a vague review into a definite pass or fail on each point.

EntradaInformation requirementsDelivered modelResultadoRequirement checklist
2

Check the model against them

Revisión del modelo

Work through the model checking naming, classification codes, level of detail and the required properties on each element type against the requirement.

Por que: A missing classification or an empty property field breaks the takeoff and the asset handover downstream. Catching it at delivery is far cheaper than finding it when it is needed.

EntradaRequirement checklistResultadoFindings against each
3

Raise an issue per gap

Incidencias del modelo

Raise a BCF issue for every gap against a requirement, pointing at the element and naming what is missing, so the author has a precise list to fix.

Por que: A precise issue gets fixed; a vague complaint that the model is not compliant gets argued. Tying each issue to a requirement and an element removes the room for debate.

EntradaFindingsResultadoBCF issues raised
4

Sign off what passes

Matriz EIR (ISO 19650)

Sign off a requirement only once the model actually meets it, so the matrix records true acceptance rather than a hopeful tick.

Por que: Sign-off is the gate that protects everyone downstream. Holding it until the model really complies is what stops a data gap being inherited by the estimator and the operator.

EntradaResolved issuesResultadoRequirement signed off
