Consegna e ciclo di vita

Run a soft landings performance handover

Do not walk away at practical completion: confirm the in-use performance targets, set up an aftercare period, tune the systems in occupation and review real performance against target through the first year.

4 passaggi11 minImpresa generaleProprietario / gestore (FM)Project e construction management

Come funziona, passo dopo passo

4 passi attraverso la piattaforma - cosa fare a ciascuno e perché conta.

1

Confirm the performance targets

Messa in servizio

Confirm the targets the building is meant to hit in use - energy, comfort, air quality - from the design intent and the commissioning results, so aftercare has something concrete to measure against.

Perché: You cannot tune towards a target nobody wrote down. Fixing the in-use performance measures at handover is what turns aftercare from a vague promise into a job with a finish line.

In entrataDesign intentCommissioning resultsIn uscitaIn-use targets
2

Set up the aftercare

Handover

Set up the aftercare period with the team who stays involved after handover, the point of contact for the occupier and the schedule of reviews through the first year.

Perché: The occupier learns a new building slowly and hits problems the delivery team can fix in minutes. A named aftercare contact is what stops small niggles turning into a soured relationship.

In entrataIn-use targetsIn uscitaAftercare plan
3

Tune the systems in use

Servizio e manutenzione

Respond to how the building is actually used: adjust the controls and setpoints, resolve the early service issues and act on occupier feedback as it comes in.

Perché: Systems commissioned to an empty building are wrong the day it fills up. Tuning them against real occupancy is where most of the promised energy and comfort performance is actually won.

In entrataAftercare planOccupier feedbackIn uscitaTuned systems
4

Review against target

Handover

Review the building's real energy and comfort performance against the targets across the first year and capture the lessons for the next project.

Perché: Measuring performance in use closes the loop that most projects leave open. It proves the building does what was promised and turns one job's hard lessons into the next one's better design.
