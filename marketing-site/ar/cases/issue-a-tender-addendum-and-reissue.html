Issue a tender addendum and reissue

A bidder query or a design change lands mid-tender. Capture it, draft the addendum, reissue it to every bidder on the same terms and confirm they all have it before the clock runs out.

4 خطوة9 دقيقةمقاول عاماستشاري تكاليف / مساح كمياتمدير مشروع / مدير إنشاء

كيف يعمل، خطوة بخطوة

4 خطوات عبر المنصة - ما تفعله في كل منها ولماذا يهم.

1

Capture what forces it

Correspondence

Log the bidder question or the design change that makes the issued information wrong or incomplete, and decide whether it needs a formal addendum to all or a private answer to one.

لماذا: The moment a query changes the scope, everyone needs to know. Capturing the trigger cleanly is what stops a helpful side conversation turning into an unfair advantage.

المدخلاتBidder queryDesign changeالمخرجاتLogged trigger
2

Draft the addendum

المستندات

Write the addendum with a clear number, the change it makes, the documents it supersedes and the revised files attached, so a bidder can act on it without guessing.

لماذا: A numbered addendum with the superseded documents named is what keeps the tender set unambiguous. Loose emails leave firms pricing off different revisions of the same drawing.

المدخلاتLogged triggerRevised documentsالمخرجاتNumbered addendum
3

Reissue to every bidder

المناقصات

Send the addendum to the whole bidder list at once and, if the change is material, extend the submission deadline so nobody is squeezed for the time to reprice.

لماذا: Issuing to all on the same day and the same terms is the heart of a fair tender. Extending the deadline where it matters keeps the prices sharp instead of rushed.

المدخلاتNumbered addendumBidder listالمخرجاتReissued to allReset deadline
4

Confirm every bidder has it

المناقصات

Track that each bidder acknowledged the addendum, and chase the ones who have not, so no firm submits against out-of-date information.

لماذا: A bid priced without the latest addendum is worthless and has to be rejected. Confirming receipt from all is the cheap step that protects the whole exercise.

المدخلاتReissued addendumالمخرجاتAcknowledged by all
